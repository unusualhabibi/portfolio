About

A legal mind
in formation

I am a law student at Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife — one of Nigeria's foremost institutions, where I enrolled in 2023. My legal education has given me a strong foundation in doctrinal law, while my independent research has taken me into the most dynamic and consequential fields of 21st-century legal practice.

My interests converge at a single point: law at the intersection of technology, globalization, and regulatory change. I believe the next generation of lawyers must be fluent not just in statutes and case law, but in policy analysis, emerging technology frameworks, and cross-border legal thinking.

I have had the privilege of interning at Clifford Chance and Mastercard — experiences that deepened my understanding of global legal practice, financial services regulation, and how elite institutions approach complex cross-border mandates.

This portfolio is a living document. It is the essays I write to synthesize complex ideas, the mini-projects I build to stress-test theory, and the timelines that show how my thinking evolves. Honest about what is still in progress.
